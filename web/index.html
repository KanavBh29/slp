ℹ️ About this project

Speech is transcribed by the browser's speech recogniser (or an in-browser Whisper model). The text is normalised, car/brand/number entities are masked, and a Bidirectional LSTM with GloVe embeddings, trained in TensorFlow/Keras, classifies it into one of 26 intents. The answer is built from a curated database of 35 supercars & hypercars.

All inference runs locally in your browser. Prices are approximate.
